# the agent

## what cy is

cy is the mind over a neuron's book. the [[neuron]] and its [[cybergraph]] exist without cy: a book holds files, links, tokens and focus and speaks no language of its own. [[neural]] is what can be said over the book. cy is what says it: it reads, decides, proposes, speaks, keeps and holds, and it is the only thing in the stack that is smart.

three things follow.

- the book needs identity, never a mind. cy is optional to a book and never a dependency of it.
- the language needs a book, never an agent. a dialect is defined without cy and executed by it.
- a person at a shell can stand where the mind stands. cy reads the same [[cy/specs/soul|soul]], asks the same [[cy/specs/ward|ward]] and writes the same signals as a model does.

## the boundary

cy works over ssh with no display. an organ that needs a screen, a camera or a speaker belongs to [[cyb]], which is cy with a body: the eyes, the ears and the pictures. the other side of the line is the stack below: cy depends on the book and the language and on nothing above; no organ of cy may require [[soft3]] to be reachable to start (the wire is optional, the book is not).

## the minimum agent

the smallest cy that is an agent and not a wallet or a database, in the order it comes alive:

1. a key and a name: a [[neuron]] with its [[cy/specs/vault|vault]] sealed under a secret.
2. a declaration: a [[cy/specs/soul|soul]] naming a model, the dialects it may speak and what it may do unasked; [[cy/specs/ward|ward]] enforces it.
3. an input: [[cy/chroma/com|com]], one line with completion.
4. a mind: [[cy/specs/soma|soma]] answers a question over the book, calling a model through glia, and links the answer back.
5. a signal out: [[cy/chroma/voice|voice]] forms it, [[cy/chroma/sense|sense]] sends it.
6. a balance and a history: sigma shows what the agent holds, [[cy/chroma/log|log]] records what it did.
7. a next move: seer proposes the cyberlink worth making; [[cy/chroma/plan|plan]] holds the standing orders.

where each stands today is in [anatomy](anatomy.md). the inference path (steps 3 to 4) and the vault, log and body are live; the soul as a file, sense as a full inbox and voice as a signal former are to build.

## the standard reflexes (proposed)

an agent that must be programmed before its first useful day is not an agent. cy ships with a set of reflexes, rune cells the owner may replace or extend:

- a new file arrives: classify it, link it to the words it means.
- a question is asked: read the soul, ask soma, link the answer.
- an epoch closes: collect the tickets the body mined and settle them.
- a follow arrives: answer with the address record.

the set is not built. its shape is a contract the [[neural]] dialects must be able to express.

## open

- soma may become a product of its own when it can run over a book that has no cy in it; the ladder then gains a rung between the language and the agent, and cy becomes the self that signs.
- ward is enforced by cy today; whether "what may be done" is itself a dialect of neural is undecided.
- the code home: `cyb/cli` and `cyb/crates/cyb/src/bin/cy.rs` until the specs settle.
