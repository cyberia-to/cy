# cy specifications

| specification | scope |
|---|---|
| [agent](agent.md) | the contract of cy: what it is, the boundary, the minimum agent, the standard reflexes |
| [anatomy](anatomy.md) | the sixteen organs, one row each: what it is and where it stands today |
| [chroma](../chroma/README.md) | the shape of the agent — one component: the 3×3 grid of the eight organs that show; their pages and code live in `chroma/` |

one page per organ, named by the anatomy. the eight that show live in [`chroma/`](../chroma/README.md): now · voice · name · sense · sigma · memory · com · time (log, plan). the unseen stay here: [[cy/specs/soma|soma]] · [[cy/specs/soul|soul]] · [[cy/specs/ward|ward]] · [[cy/specs/vault|vault]] · [[cy/specs/state|state]] · [[cy/specs/body|body]]; glia and seer are their own repositories.

soma, glia, seer and vault are unseen organs with repositories of their own; their pages here are the rows of the anatomy.

this repository holds contracts. audits, benchmarks and release receipts go to `audit/` at the root, per the shared rule in `soft3/specs/README.md`. the ladder, the parts registry and the vocabulary live in [soft3](https://github.com/cyberia-to/soft3) and are not restated here.
