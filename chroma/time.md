# time

one screen: [[cy/chroma/log|log]] ← **now** → [[cy/chroma/plan|plan]] — the past flowing into the present flowing into the schedule, with [[cy/chroma/now|now]] in the middle. your life on one axis.

time is a [[cy/chroma/README|chroma]] organ (bottom-right) with two halves: **log**, the history of every interaction, and **plan**, the standing orders and deferred intents. now is not inside time — it is the hinge the whole agent pivots on; time is the organ that lays the two directions of it side by side. headless, it is a scrollable pane; in [[cyb]] it is the flagship view.
